Crystal College — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What are the tuition fee deadlines?
A: Tuition fees for the autumn semester are due by August 15. Spring semester fees are due by January 15. Late payments incur a 2% per month penalty. Students can pay online through the student portal or at the finance office in Block B.

Q: How do I register for courses?
A: Course registration opens two weeks before the semester begins. Log in to the Student Portal at portal.crystal.edu, navigate to "Course Registration," select your courses, and click "Submit Registration." Each student can register for a maximum of 6 courses per semester.

Q: What is the attendance policy?
A: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course. If attendance falls below 75%, students are not permitted to sit for final examinations. Medical leave requires a doctor's certificate submitted to the Academic Office within 3 days of absence.

Q: How do I apply for financial aid or scholarships?
A: Financial aid applications are accepted from June 1 to July 31 for the upcoming academic year. Visit the Student Finance Office (Block B, Room 102) or download the application form from the college website. Merit scholarships require a minimum GPA of 3.5.

Q: What are the library hours?
A: The main library is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and closed on Sundays and public holidays. E-resources are available 24/7 through the library portal.

Q: How do I contact academic support services?
A: The Academic Support Centre is in Block A, Room 205. Email: academic.support@crystal.edu. Phone: +91-44-2345-6789. Walk-in hours: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 10 AM to 4 PM.

Q: What documents do I need for admission?
A: You need: (1) 10th and 12th grade mark sheets, (2) Transfer certificate, (3) Migration certificate (if from another state), (4) Character certificate, (5) Two passport-size photographs, (6) Aadhaar card copy, (7) Category certificate (if applicable).

Q: How do I apply for hostel accommodation?
A: Hostel applications open on May 1 each year. Submit the hostel application form along with Rs. 500 registration fee to the Hostel Office (Block D). Allotment is based on distance from hometown and academic performance. Capacity is limited.

Q: What is the grading system?
A: Crystal College uses a 10-point grading scale. O (Outstanding) = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8, B+ = 7, B = 6, C = 5. A minimum grade of C is required to pass a course. Students with less than 5.0 CGPA are placed on academic probation.

Q: How do I get a transcript or certificate?
A: Transcripts and certificates can be requested at the Registrar's Office (Block C, Room 101). Processing time is 5 working days for regular requests and 1 working day for urgent requests (additional fee applies). Official transcripts are sealed and stamped.

Q: What sports and extracurricular activities are available?
A: Crystal College offers cricket, football, basketball, badminton, chess, and athletics. Cultural clubs include Music Society, Drama Club, Photography Club, and Debate Society. Registration happens in the first two weeks of each semester at the Student Activities Office.

Q: How do I raise a grievance?
A: Student grievances can be submitted through the online Grievance Portal at portal.crystal.edu/grievance, or in writing to the Student Welfare Officer (Block A, Room 110). All grievances are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 15 working days.

Q: Is there a canteen or food service on campus?
A: The main canteen in Block E serves breakfast (7:30-9:30 AM), lunch (12:00-2:30 PM), and dinner (6:30-9:00 PM). A vegetarian food court is also available in Block A. All food handlers are certified, and hygiene inspections are conducted monthly.

Q: What IT and technology resources are available?
A: The campus has Wi-Fi coverage in all buildings. Computer labs are open from 8 AM to 9 PM. Each student gets 10 GB cloud storage on the college server. Microsoft Office 365 licenses are provided free of charge. For IT support, email: itsupport@crystal.edu.

Q: How do I apply for an internship?
A: The Placement Cell (Block F, Room 201) coordinates internships. Register your interest in the Student Portal by October 15 (for summer internships). The cell has partnerships with 150+ companies. Minimum CGPA requirement for placement assistance is 6.0.

Q: What health services are available on campus?
A: The Health Centre in Block G operates Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. An emergency nurse is on duty 24/7 in the hostel. Basic medicines are provided free of charge. For serious conditions, students are referred to City General Hospital (2 km from campus).

Q: How are supplementary or re-examinations handled?
A: Students who fail a course (grade below C) may apply for a supplementary exam within 7 days of result publication. Supplementary exams are held 3 weeks after final exams. A fee of Rs. 500 per subject applies. Maximum two supplementary attempts are allowed per course.

Q: What is the dress code policy?
A: Business casual attire is required in academic buildings on weekdays. Lab sessions require appropriate safety attire (lab coats, closed-toe shoes). Traditional wear is permitted on cultural event days. Offensive or inappropriate clothing is not permitted.

Q: How do I get a student ID card?
A: New student ID cards are issued during orientation week. For replacement cards (lost/damaged), visit the Admin Office (Block C, Room 103) with a police FIR (for lost cards) or the damaged card. Replacement fee is Rs. 200. Processing takes 3 working days.

Q: What is the policy on mobile phones and electronic devices in class?
A: Mobile phones must be set to silent mode during all lectures, labs, and examinations. Usage of phones for recording lectures requires prior written permission from the faculty. Devices may be confiscated if used inappropriately during class sessions.
